The Real Problem

Where Booked Consults Are Slipping Away

SmileSketch Vegas is a young, modern practice competing in one of the most crowded cosmetic-dentistry markets in the country. The aesthetic is dialed in — the follow-up system isn't yet. Here's where high-intent patients are quietly falling through.

🏛️

A Young Practice Still Building Trust

Incorporated in 2022 and not yet BBB-accredited, SmileSketch Vegas is competing against decades-old Vegas cosmetic practices. Every inquiry that goes unanswered is trust that's harder to rebuild the second time around.

📱

Social-Driven Inquiries, No Capture System

Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest are actively driving DMs and comments asking about veneers and smile makeovers — but without a structured intake, those messages sit unread between appointments.

⏰

Slow Response Loses High-Ticket Cases

A patient asking about a $15K–$30K smile makeover is almost always comparing 2–3 practices at once. Whoever replies first — with a clear next step — usually wins the consult.

🦷

Long Decision Cycles Go Cold

Veneers, implants, and full smile makeovers are rarely decided on the spot. Without a nurture sequence, prospects who don't book immediately after their first inquiry quietly disappear.

📵

Missed Calls & No-Shows, No Recovery

There's no visible system to catch a missed call or a no-show consult and bring that patient back into the pipeline — each one is a fully-marketed lead going to waste.

⭐

No Systemized Review & Referral Engine

Word-of-mouth and 5-star reviews build credibility faster than any ad spend — but without automatic prompts after great results, most happy patients never get asked.
