The Real Problem

Where Boston's Rental Volume Is Leaking Revenue

As VP of Sales, you've built the visibility and listing volume that Boston renters, landlords, and investors already trust. The gap isn't demand — it's what happens in the minutes after someone reaches out.

⚡

Renters Move to the Next Listing Fast

In a market this competitive, a renter who doesn't hear back in minutes simply messages the next listing on the page. Every slow reply is a showing handed to a competitor.

📥

Inquiry Volume Outpaces Manual Triage

Hundreds of rental inquiries a day across listings, portals, and phone lines is more than any team can manually sort, qualify, and route in real time — so good leads sit unanswered.

🏢

Investor & Multifamily Leads Go Cold

Landlord and multifamily investor inquiries need a longer, more consultative follow-up than a rental — but without a dedicated nurture track, they get the same one-touch treatment and quietly disappear.

📅

No Automated Showing Booking

Scheduling and confirming showings still runs through manual back-and-forth, which means avoidable no-shows and wasted agent time on units that are showing themselves to no one.

🔁

Past Clients & Landlords Never Re-Engaged

Tenants who signed a lease and landlords who filled a unit rarely hear from Boston Pads again — a lost channel for repeat listings, renewals, and referrals that costs nothing to open back up.

👥

Inconsistent Team Follow-Up

Without a shared system, each agent follows up their own way — some fast, some not at all — so lead quality and conversion swing wildly depending on who happens to answer.
