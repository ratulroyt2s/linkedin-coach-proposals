The Real Problem

Where Dentalia Is Leaking Bookings

Running general dentistry and facial aesthetics under one name means double the inquiry types, double the follow-up work, and double the chances something falls through — all handled by hand today.

🦷

Two Patient Journeys, One Inbox

A dental check-up enquiry and a dermal filler enquiry need completely different responses, forms, and follow-up cadences — but both land in the same inbox with no system to route or prioritize them.

📱

Aesthetics Inquiries Are Urgency-Sensitive

Wrinkle-reduction and filler enquiries are often social-media-driven and time-sensitive — the prospective patient is usually messaging two or three practitioners at once. Whoever replies first tends to win the booking.

🔁

No Cross-Sell Into Aesthetics

Existing dental patients who already trust Dr. O'Kill are a warm audience for facial aesthetics — but there's no system currently introducing them to those services at the right moment.

🗂️

Fragmented Directory Presence

Listings across Checkup.co.uk, Dentist Finder, and other directories bring in inquiries from multiple disconnected channels, with no unified place to track or follow up on any of them.

💉

High-Touch Treatments, Manual Nurture

Fillers and wrinkle-reduction treatments benefit from careful pre-treatment prep and post-treatment aftercare guidance — currently sent manually, if at all, instead of on a consistent automated schedule.

📅

No-Shows on High-Value Appointments

Without a structured reminder sequence, consultations for both cosmetic dentistry and aesthetics treatments are vulnerable to no-shows — a costly gap for chair time that's already in high demand.

💉 Treatment & Onboarding Automation

Pre-treatment prep and post-treatment aftercare are delivered automatically and on time — essential for fillers, wrinkle-reduction, and dental procedures alike.

📄

Consent Forms & Pre-Treatment Instructions

Sent automatically once a treatment is booked — tailored to the procedure, so nothing is missed before the appointment

🔔

Reminder Sequence With Prep Guidance

e.g. "avoid blood thinners before your filler appointment" — timed reminders with treatment-specific guidance

💳

Payment & Deposit Automation

Deposit or payment link sent on booking confirmation, reducing last-minute cancellations on high-value treatments

🩹

Post-Treatment Aftercare & Check-In
